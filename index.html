About Sporing

A foraging chance map for England. Pick a mushroom and a month — the hex map shows where that species is most likely to be fruiting, based on the season, typical habitat and climate. Your home pin is Barnsley, South Yorkshire.

How the chance is calculated

  • Season — each species has a monthly fruiting curve (morels peak in April–May, oysters run through autumn).
  • Habitat — weighted hotspots where the species is classically found (heath & conifers for chanterelles, beech woods for hedgehogs, chalk downland for field mushrooms…).
  • Climate — a gentle latitude adjustment.
  • It is a model estimate, not field reports — treat it as a guide to when and where to look, not a guarantee.

Foraging responsibly

  • Always confirm identification with more than one reliable source before eating anything. Mis-ID is the real risk in the wild.
  • Log only mushrooms you have actually found, and only species you're sure of.
  • Check landowner permission, stay off SSSIs, and follow local rules on public rights of way.
  • Take a little, leave the mycelium alive — the mushrooms you don't pick are next year's.
